About the institute

The MacGuffin Realm Institute of Archival Historians, among other names, depending on your realm, is an organisation dedicated to investigating and researching the myriad realms that surround our own in the sea of universes. The institute is a multi-realm spanning organisation, comprising millions of archival teams. Each archival team is tightly curated, and specialises on one specific realm, each of whose websites are made by the teams. Contact realmadmin@contact.macguff.org for job openings or overarching enquiries or grievances. For matters of one specific realm or archival team, head to Contact or the realm team's specific contact page.
